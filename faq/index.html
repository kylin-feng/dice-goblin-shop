FAQ

Everything adventurers ask before they adopt a hoard.

Metal or resin — which should I get?

Metal dice feel amazing and roll with authority — best paired with the folding tray (they are heavy enough to dent a bare table). Resin sets are light, table-friendly and sharper-edged for the price. Most players end up with one of each: resin for the tavern, metal for the boss fight.

Are metal dice safe for my table?

Not on bare wood — that is exactly why the bundle includes a folding tray. Rolling metal dice in the tray protects both the table and the dice's finish.

How do I care for metal dice?

Keep them dry, wipe occasionally with a soft cloth, and store them in the pouch. Avoid leaving them in a damp backpack; the plating does not like long baths.
