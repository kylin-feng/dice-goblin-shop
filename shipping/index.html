Shipping Policy

Free worldwide shipping on every order — here is exactly how it moves.

Processing

Orders are packed within 2–4 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays). You receive a tracking number by email the moment your parcel is handed to the carrier.

Delivery times (after dispatch)

  • United States: 7–15 business days
  • United Kingdom & European Union: 8–18 business days
  • Canada & Australia: 10–20 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–25 business days

Tracking

Every parcel ships with a trackable service. Tracking activates within 48–72 hours of dispatch (the first carrier scan can lag behind the label creation — that is normal).

Customs & import duties

Parcels are dispatched from China and declared honestly at order value. Most destinations do not charge import duty on low-value parcels like dice sets; where your country does, any import tax is the buyer's responsibility as per local law.

Lost or stuck parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 30 days, contact us — we reship or refund, your choice. Your purchase is also covered by PayPal buyer protection.
