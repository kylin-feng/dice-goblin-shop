Returns & Refunds

You may return any unused, complete set within 14 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Message us first via the contact page so we can expect the parcel; return shipping is at the buyer's cost.

Photo the damage and the packaging, send it to us within 7 days of delivery, and we ship a free replacement — no need to return the damaged set.

Mistakes happen (the goblin gets excited). We reship the missing piece or the correct set at our cost.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business days of the return being accepted (or immediately for transit damage); PayPal typically posts them within 1–5 business days.
